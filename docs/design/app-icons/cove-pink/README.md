# Cove: ten pink icon variations

Ten additional concepts, preserving the previous Cove SVGs. Editable 1024 × 1024 SVGs, full-bleed backgrounds, no fonts or external dependencies. Rounded corners are preview-only; platform masks and Android adaptive layers require final packaging.

Pink palette: #ff657e, #f13c66, #d91f55; marks: #fff9fa and #ffc5d4.

- **01 — Tidal C** (`cove-pink-01.svg`): An open C with a wave flowing through its centre.
- **02 — Play inlet** (`cove-pink-02.svg`): A broad shoreline opens directly into the play symbol.
- **03 — Curl** (`cove-pink-03.svg`): A sculpted wave curls into a single, unmistakable cove.
- **04 — Twin tides** (`cove-pink-04.svg`): Two parallel contours trace a sheltered shoreline.
- **05 — Lagoon** (`cove-pink-05.svg`): A circular lagoon with a quiet wave cut through it.
- **06 — Silk current** (`cove-pink-06.svg`): A broad ribbon curves around an open centre.
- **07 — Sound haven** (`cove-pink-07.svg`): Three sound pulses held within a protective C.
- **08 — Harbour** (`cove-pink-08.svg`): An embracing bay beneath a rolling white crest.
- **09 — Wave note** (`cove-pink-09.svg`): A musical note with a flowing, wave-shaped flag.
- **10 — Continuous coast** (`cove-pink-10.svg`): One continuous line joins the shore and its wave.
