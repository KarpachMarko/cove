# App icon concepts

Ten original, editable SVG icons: two each for Zen Music, Zen Library, Cove, Cadence, and Undertone. Open index.html for the comparison sheet, or open an individual SVG directly.

All icons have a 1024 × 1024 viewBox, vector geometry, embedded gradients, and no fonts, external images, scripts, or dependencies. The square backgrounds are full bleed; rounded corners are only applied in the preview. Native iOS and Android packaging should apply each platform’s mask. Android adaptive foreground/background layers and store-specific exports are not included.

- **Zen Music A — Flowing Z** (`zen-music-a.svg`): A continuous ribbon turns the Z into a quiet musical gesture.
- **Zen Music B — Quiet rhythm** (`zen-music-b.svg`): An open brush-like ring shelters three restrained sound pulses.
- **Zen Library A — Record shelf** (`zen-library-a.svg`): Two upright spines and one tilted sleeve describe a personal collection.
- **Zen Library B — Sleeve & spindle** (`zen-library-b.svg`): A record peeking from its sleeve gives the library a physical identity.
- **Cove A — Sound inlet** (`cove-a.svg`): A C-shaped shoreline opens around a play symbol.
- **Cove B — Sheltered wave** (`cove-b.svg`): A single rolling wave sits inside the protected curve of a bay.
- **Cadence A — Measured rhythm** (`cadence-a.svg`): Five rounded beats form an ascending and falling musical phrase.
- **Cadence B — Looping phrase** (`cadence-b.svg`): Two linked arches suggest a repeated phrase with a rising cadence.
- **Undertone A — Resonant U** (`undertone-a.svg`): A grounded U floats above a low sustained bass line.
- **Undertone B — Below the surface** (`undertone-b.svg`): An interrupted horizon reveals a deep curve and its returning echo.
